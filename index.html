Good questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything we get asked before a Wollongong business signs on with prospr.

What is Google Business Profile optimisation?
Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the listing that shows up in Google Maps. Optimisation involves updating your categories, service areas, photos, keywords, and review strategies so that Google positions your business in the "Local 3-Pack" — the top three map results people see first.
Why shouldn't I just run my own Facebook or Google ads?
You absolutely can, but managing ad platforms efficiently requires constant tracking and technical optimisation. Without proper setup, it's incredibly easy to waste hundreds of dollars on broad keywords or incorrect geographic targeting. We save you time and make sure every dollar of your ad spend works as hard as possible.
Should I choose Google Ads or Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads?
It depends entirely on your industry and target market. Google Ads are ideal for "high-intent" searches (e.g., someone typing "emergency plumber near me"). Meta Ads are excellent for visual businesses or offer-driven services. We'll review your business and give you a clear recommendation.
Can we meet face to face?
Genuinely, yes. If you're located in Wollongong, Shellharbour, or across the broader Illawarra, you don't have to settle for endless video calls. We're happy to sit down over a coffee or meet you at your office to talk through your strategy.
What if I've been burnt by an agency before?
You own everything from day one — full access to your files, your domain, and your data. We don't hold anything hostage. If you ever decide to leave, everything comes with you, no arguments.
I'm not a "tech person." How complicated is this for me?
We built prospr explicitly for busy business owners who hate messing with tech. We handle all the setup, coding, tracking, and troubleshooting. You just answer the phone and look at your simplified monthly report.
Do you only work with businesses in Wollongong?
Wollongong and the surrounding Illawarra is our core focus and where most of our clients are based, but we do work with businesses further afield where it makes sense.
Are there any hidden fees or setup costs?
Transparency is one of our core values — there are no hidden fees. Everything is quoted upfront, so what you agree to is what you pay.
How long does a new website take?
Most sites are live within a week of us receiving your content. We provide a simple content checklist upfront so there are no delays on our end.
Why do you focus so heavily on website speed and mobile optimisation?
The vast majority of local searches happen on smartphones, often by customers who want something sorted fast. If your website takes more than a couple of seconds to load, or looks clunky on a mobile screen, prospects will tap out and call your competitor. We engineer every site from the ground up to load instantly and function flawlessly on mobile devices.
What does "plus ad spend" mean?
We'll give you a quote for our management fee — the work we do to build, monitor, and optimise your ad campaigns. Your "ad spend" is the actual budget that goes directly to Google or Meta to display your ads. You pay this budget directly to the ad platforms, and we help you determine a budget that fits your goals.
How long does it take to see results from Local SEO?
Paid ads can start generating leads within days of launching. Organic Local SEO is a long-term asset; it typically takes 3 to 6 months of consistent optimisation to see sustainable moves in search rankings. However, these organic leads cost you nothing once you rank.
Do you force me to move my website hosting to you?
Not at all. If you already have a reliable, fast hosting provider you're happy with, we can build or optimise your website right where it is. We offer secure, high-speed hosting as a convenience to keep everything under one roof, but where your digital assets live is entirely up to you.
What's the minimum commitment?
A website build is a one-time project. Care plans, ads, and SEO are all month-to-month, with no lock-in contracts. We'd rather earn your business every month than trap you into staying.
What if I already have a website?
We'll audit it first — speed, mobile experience, and where it's likely losing enquiries. If it's salvageable, we'll tell you honestly. If it's actively costing you jobs, we'll show you exactly why, with data rather than opinion.
How much does a new website cost?
It depends on the scope of the project. Rather than guess, we'd rather have a quick conversation about what you actually need and give you a clear, itemised quote with no hidden extras.
Can you help if I'm not sure what I actually need?
Yes — a quick, free digital health check is usually the fastest way to work out whether the issue is your website, your visibility, or something else entirely.
What's included in a care plan?
Hosting, security monitoring, plugin and theme updates, and regular backups. Your site stays fast, safe, and online without you having to think about it.
